I’m Yash Gawade, a designer and developer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, interactive digital experiences. This is where I’ll write about what I’m learning while building projects that challenge me.

## What you’ll find here

- Notes on UI/UX and frontend development.
- Write-ups of full-stack projects as they take shape.
- Experiments with AI in my workflow.

## How this site is built

The site is a Next.js app written in TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui, with Motion for the small animations. Everything on the home page is made of reusable pieces, which you can browse on the [Components](/components) and [Blocks](/blocks) pages.

Posts like this one are plain MDX files, so adding a new one is just a matter of dropping a file into the content folder.
